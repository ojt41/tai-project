# Nordvik Electronics: Shipping and Delivery Policy

## Delivery times
Orders placed before 14:00 on a working day are shipped the same day. Delivery within Finland takes 1 to 3 working days. We currently deliver only to addresses in Finland.

## Shipping costs
Standard delivery costs 5.90 euros. Orders over 100 euros, after discounts, ship free of charge. Express delivery to the next working day costs 14.90 euros and is available in the Helsinki region only.

## Damaged or missing deliveries
If a parcel arrives damaged or an item is missing, report it through "My orders" within 48 hours of delivery and include photos of the parcel and the item. Reports made after 48 hours cannot be accepted for shipping damage, but faulty items can still be handled under the Faulty items rules of the Returns and Refunds Policy.

## Tracking
A tracking link is sent by email when the order ships. If tracking shows no movement for 5 working days, contact customer service and we will open an investigation with the carrier.
