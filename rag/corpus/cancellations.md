# Nordvik Electronics: Order Cancellation Policy

## Cancelling before shipping
Orders can be cancelled free of charge until they have been shipped. To cancel, open "My orders" in your account and choose "Cancel order". The order status shows "Processing" while cancellation is still possible. The full amount, including shipping, is refunded within 5 working days.

## Cancelling after shipping
Once an order has the status "Shipped", it can no longer be cancelled. Instead, the customer can refuse the delivery or return the item under the Returns and Refunds Policy, where the 30-day return window and the unused-item condition apply.

## Custom and pre-ordered items
Custom-configured computers can be cancelled free of charge within 24 hours of ordering. After 24 hours, a cancellation fee of 15% of the order value applies, because assembly has started. Pre-ordered products can be cancelled free of charge at any time before they are shipped.

## Repeated or partial cancellations
Individual items in a multi-item order can be cancelled separately before the order ships. Nordvik Electronics may refuse new orders from accounts that have cancelled more than five orders within 30 days.
