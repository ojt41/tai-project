# Nordvik Electronics: Discounts and Promotions Policy

## Student discount
Students get 10% off full-price items after verifying their student status through the "Student" page in their account. Verification is valid for 12 months. The student discount does not apply to sale items, gift cards, or custom-configured computers.

## Promo codes
Promo codes are published in our newsletter and on our website. Only one promo code can be used per order, and promo codes cannot be combined with the student discount. A promo code must be entered before payment; it cannot be applied to an order after it has been placed.

## Sale and clearance items
Items marked "Sale" or "Clearance" are sold at a reduced price and are final sale. No further discounts, promo codes, or student discounts can be applied to them.

## Price match
If a product goes on sale on our own website within 7 days of your purchase, we refund the price difference once on request. We do not match prices from other stores or marketplaces.

## Loyalty points
Members earn 1 loyalty point per euro spent on full-price items. 100 points can be redeemed for a 5 euro voucher. Points expire 12 months after they are earned and have no cash value. Points are removed if the purchase that earned them is returned.
