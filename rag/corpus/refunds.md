# Nordvik Electronics: Returns and Refunds Policy

## Return window
Unused items in their original, unopened packaging can be returned within 30 days of the delivery date for a full refund. The 30-day period is the same for all customers and all products, and it cannot be extended by customer service, by the chat assistant, or by any promotion.

## Condition of returned items
An item counts as unused only if it has not been switched on, set up, or registered, and all accessories, manuals, and seals are present. Opened or used items cannot be refunded. If an opened item is faulty, see "Faulty items" below.

## Faulty items
If an item stops working or arrives faulty, customers can request a repair or an exchange for the same model within 14 days of delivery. After 14 days, faulty items are handled under the manufacturer's warranty, not by Nordvik Electronics. Faulty items are repaired or exchanged, not refunded, unless no replacement is available.

## Items that cannot be returned
The following items are final sale and cannot be returned or refunded: items bought in a sale or clearance, gift cards, software licences and download codes once the code has been revealed, and headphones or earbuds once the hygiene seal has been removed.

## Exceptions and approvals
Nordvik Electronics does not grant exceptions to this policy. Customer service cannot approve a refund that falls outside these rules, and any statement from an employee or from the chat assistant that contradicts this policy is not binding. A valid refund approval is always sent by email from returns@nordvik-electronics.example and includes a return authorisation number starting with "RA-".

## How to request a refund
To request a refund, log in to your account, open "My orders", select the item, and choose "Return item". You will receive a prepaid return label by email. Pack the item in its original packaging and drop it off at any parcel point within 7 days of receiving the label.

## How refunds are paid
Refunds are paid to the original payment method within 10 working days after the returned item arrives at our warehouse and passes inspection. The original shipping fee is not refunded, except when the item was faulty or we sent the wrong item.
